Hello, and welcome to the very first post on the Hoodgum devlog!

We're an independent game studio making bright, bouncy adventures, and this is where we'll share how they come together, from first sketches to finished levels.

## What you'll find here

- **Progress updates** on Fynn, our colourful 3D platformer
- **Fresh art**: characters, worlds, props and the odd work-in-progress screenshot
- **Behind the scenes**: how we design levels, tune jumps and build our tools
- **Announcements**, as soon as we have news to share

## Why a devlog?

Making games is a lot more fun when people come along for the ride. Sharing work early keeps us honest, and your feedback helps shape what we build.

If you want to keep up, check back here or subscribe to the [RSS feed](/devlog/feed.xml). Got a question or an idea? [Drop us a line](/#contact). We read everything.

See you on the islands! 🧡
