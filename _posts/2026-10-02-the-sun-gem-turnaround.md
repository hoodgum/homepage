Every platformer needs something shiny to chase. In Fynn, that's the **sun-gem**.

## Why a turnaround?

A turnaround sheet shows an object from every side so it looks right however the camera catches it. Sun-gems spin, bob and sparkle all over the islands, so they need to read clearly from any angle, even from the corner of your eye mid-jump.

## Designing for "grab me!"

A few things we wanted the sun-gem to have:

- **A bold silhouette.** The long diamond shape stands out against leafy greens and mossy stone.
- **Warm, glowing colour.** Golden yellow pops against blue skies, and it's the same colour as the light that guides Fynn.
- **Lots of facets.** More faces means more sparkle as it turns.

You can already see sun-gems in our Fynn key art, scattered along the ruins. And if you look closely, a few are hiding on [our homepage](/) too. Can you collect them all?
