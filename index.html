Fynn

Run, swing and glide your way back to the light.

Fynn is a colourful 3D platformer about a laid-back hero chasing the stolen Cinderheart through winding forests, canyons and enemy camps. Run, glide, wall-run and swing through bright, layered worlds full of secrets.

They came in the night.
Fynn is going to get it back.

The Cinderheart is the glowing crystal that keeps the village lights burning and the water wheels turning. One night, while everyone slept, the Hoodgums crept in and carried it off.

Fynn wakes to a dark village and a trail of footprints. Behind it all is the pompous warlord Krek and his army of Hoodgums: hooded, floating-handed troublemakers who are very pleased with themselves. Fynn is going to change that.

Fynn is a fast, playful 3D platformer built around movement that feels good. Sprint down winding forest trails, leap between ledges, glide over rivers, run along walls and swing from ring to ring across canyon gaps. Every level is packed with shortcuts, secret branches and upper and lower routes that reward curiosity.

The world is bright, hand-modelled and full of character, from sleepy lantern-lit villages to mossy canyons and Hoodgum camps. Day turns to night, lanterns glow, and something is always just around the next bend.

Features

  • 🏃Fluid movement: run, double jump, glide, wall-run, wall-kick, roll, climb and swing
  • 🗺️Winding, layered levels with forks, upper and lower routes, and hidden secrets
  • 🛡️A cast of Hoodgums to outsmart: walkers, shield bearers, bomb throwers, chargers and gunners
  • 🌙Hand-modelled, storybook-style worlds with day and night
  • 😄A light-hearted adventure that never takes itself too seriously
  • 📱Play in your browser, or on iPhone and iPad
