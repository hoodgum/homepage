Fynn

A sun-soaked 3D platforming adventure across a sky full of floating ruins.

Dash, leap and slide as Fynn, a fearless little hero with a big red scarf and even bigger shoes. Chase down shimmering sun-gems, outsmart rattling clockwork bots and uncover the secrets of the islands, with a glowing firefly friend lighting the way.

  • Floating islands
  • Sun-gems to hunt
  • Clockwork baddies
  • A firefly sidekick
